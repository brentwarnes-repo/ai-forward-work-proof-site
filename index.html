The leverage boundary

AI should absorb the repeatable work. Human attention should be reserved for the decisions where context, consequences, taste, or accountability actually change the outcome.

AI does the volume work

Prepare, transform, verify, package

  • Collect and normalize source material
  • Extract, compare, test, and flag exceptions
  • Draft evidence-backed artifacts and visuals
  • Run repeatable QA and link/claim checks
  • Return one complete review packet—not a pile of raw output

The human does the scarce work

Frame, decide, approve, release

  • Choose the outcome and define what matters
  • Resolve ambiguity that requires real context
  • Make tradeoffs with business or reputational consequences
  • Approve bounded writes and public claims
  • Make the final taste and publication decision
Human-led judgment + AI-led execution + independent verification = credible leverage

Every claim carries its status

The proof surface distinguishes what was observed, what was tested, what is only expected, and what remains unknown.

Observed

Directly recorded in source evidence or an executed run.

Validated

Confirmed by a defined test, review, or independent readback.

Expected

A reasoned hypothesis that has not yet earned a validated label.

Unknown

Not measured, not available, or deliberately outside the claim boundary.
